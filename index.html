Privacy Policy — TV Schedule

Last updated: 03/08/2026

TV Schedule (“the app”) is a television programme guide. It shows broadcast schedules for a number of countries and regions. It does not stream, host or link to any video content.

This policy explains what the app does with information. The short version: the app has no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics. It does not ask for your name, email address or location, and nothing you do in the app is sent to us.

Information the app stores on your device

The following are saved locally on your phone and are never transmitted anywhere:

  • Your selected country or region
  • Your time format preference (12- or 24-hour)
  • Your favourite channels and programmes
  • Programme reminders you have set
  • A temporary cache of guide data and app configuration, so the app works offline and loads faster

You can erase all of it at any time by clearing the app’s storage in your device settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Information sent to other companies

The app connects to third-party services to function. When it does, those services necessarily receive your device’s IP address and standard technical request information, as happens with any internet connection.

ServiceWhyWhat it receives
Programme guide providers To download broadcast schedules and channel logos IP address, request time, device/browser type
Google Firebase To fetch the app’s configuration (which countries and data sources are available) IP address, request time
Google AdMob To display advertising, which is how the app is funded Advertising ID, device information, coarse location inferred from IP, and ad interaction data

Google’s handling of this data is governed by its own policies. You can read how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and Google’s privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Advertising

The app shows banner advertising supplied by Google AdMob. Ads may be personalised based on your Advertising ID.

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time in Settings → Privacy → Ads on your Android device, which also lets you opt out of ad personalisation across all apps.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, and you can change that choice later from within the app.

Permissions the app requests

  • Internet access — required to download programme schedules and to show ads.
  • Notifications — optional. Only used to remind you about a programme you chose to be reminded about. Reminders are generated on your device; nothing is sent to a server. You can decline this permission and the rest of the app works normally.
  • Advertising ID — used by AdMob as described above.

The app does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or call history.

Children

The app is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect any information from children. Programme listings are supplied by third-party guide providers and describe general broadcast television.

Data retention and deletion

Because the app stores everything locally and holds no accounts, there is no data of yours for us to retain or delete. Removing the app removes everything it saved. To remove advertising data held by Google, use the Android advertising controls linked above.

Programme information

Schedule data and channel logos come from third-party programme guide sources. Channel names and logos are trademarks of their respective broadcasters and are shown only to identify the channel in the guide. TV Schedule is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any broadcaster.
